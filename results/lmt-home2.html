About

Who We Are

Lanka Metro Transit (Pvt) Ltd is focused on reshaping city travel with international standards of service, accessibility and safety, centered on the needs of Sri Lankan commuters.

Vision

Be Sri Lanka's most trusted and preferred urban public transport service—delivering world-class, accessible, efficient, and sustainable mobility that connects communities and supports national progress.

Mission

  • Provide accessible mobility across modern, low-floor city buses.
  • Deliver reliable, efficient services that reduce congestion and travel time.
  • Ensure comfort and safety via well-maintained fleets and trained personnel.
  • Promote environmental sustainability with reduced emissions.
